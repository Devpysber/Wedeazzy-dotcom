Frequently asked questions.

How does WedEazzy help my business? +
WedEazzy showcases your profile to active wedding couples searching for trusted vendors in your city, delivering direct enquiries straight to your WhatsApp and phone.
How do couples contact me? +
Couples can send enquiries directly to your registered WhatsApp number or call your mobile phone directly.
What happens after I purchase a plan? +
Our onboarding team contacts you to gather your business details, portfolio photos, and service information to complete your listing setup.
Do I have to create my profile myself? +
No! WedEazzy handles your profile setup and listing configuration for you once you purchase a plan.
Do you charge commission on bookings? +
No! WedEazzy charges 0% commission on any of your wedding bookings. You keep 100% of your earnings.
Can I update my business information? +
Yes, you get full access to your vendor dashboard to update photos, services, pricing, and contact details anytime.
How long does onboarding take? +
Once you provide your basic business details and portfolio photos, your profile is typically live within 24 to 48 hours.
Can I cancel my plan? +
Yes, you can manage or cancel your subscription renewal anytime from your vendor dashboard or by contacting support.
Are bookings guaranteed? +
WedEazzy provides high-visibility placement and direct customer enquiries from active couples. Final bookings depend on your portfolio, availability, and pricing.
