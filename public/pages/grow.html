FAQ

Questions Vendors Ask

What exactly is WedEazzy?

WedEazzy is a specialised platform for weddings. Couples use it to find and contact wedding businesses, and businesses use it to get discovered and receive enquiries.

What do I need to do after purchasing a plan?

Nothing technical. The WedEazzy team sets up and runs your campaign and sends enquiries to your WhatsApp and dashboard. You reply to couples and take the bookings.

Do I need my own Facebook or Instagram ad account?

No. WedEazzy runs the campaigns for you. No ad account or marketing experience is needed.

Are the lead numbers guaranteed?

They are estimates based on campaign budgets. Actual results depend on your category, city, season, pricing and how quickly you respond.

Do you take any commission on bookings?

No. You pay only for the plan. Every booking from a WedEazzy lead is fully yours.

Is the listing really free?

Yes. Registering or claiming your business is free. Premium listings and Grow campaigns are optional.
