FAQ

Good to know

Is WedEazzy free for couples?

Yes. Searching, sending enquiries and talking to vendors is completely free. You pay vendors directly — we never add booking fees.

How fast will vendors respond?

Most enquiries get matched vendor responses within 24 hours. For urgent dates, WhatsApp us and we'll prioritise.

Are vendors verified?

Listings are checked by our team for business details, contact information and reviews before they're marked verified.

Do you help with destination weddings?

Yes — we cover Goa, Udaipur, Jodhpur and international destinations like Dubai and London. Send an enquiry with your destination.

Is listing really free?

Yes. A free listing includes your profile, gallery and enquiries from couples. Paid plans are optional.

Do you take commission on bookings?

Never. Couples pay you directly and you keep 100% of every booking.

How do Grow campaigns work?

Pick a plan and our team creates and runs ads for your business. Leads come straight to your WhatsApp and you track results on your dashboard. See plans.

My business is already listed. What now?
